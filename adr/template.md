# ADR-NNNN: Short noun phrase naming the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Amended by ADR-NNNN | Superseded by ADR-NNNN | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** jka
- **Decision log:** AGENTS.md DNNN

## Context

The forces at play: what problem prompted the decision, which constraints
apply (architecture, dependencies, platforms, users), and which earlier
decisions it builds on. Write it so a reader who was not present can see why a
decision was needed at all.

## Decision

What was decided, stated in full sentences in the active voice ("The core
stores…", "rio does not…"). Include the concrete shape where it matters: the
operation names, the file locations, the defaults.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were weighed and why each was not taken. Omit the section if
there were none worth recording.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. Include the costs, the known
limits, and the follow-up work the decision creates.
